How to Conduct Survey

1

Identify Household & Surveyor

Click the "+" button at bottom. Enter your Full Name, Ward #, House ID, and Head of Household.

2

Answer Physics Energy Questions

Fill Sections 1–5: Grid Type, Lighting tech, Fan motors, AC settings, Refrigerator, and Rooftop Solar PV.

3

Submit & View Instructions

Click "Push to Cloud Server". The app will display submitted answers and personalized energy instructions.
